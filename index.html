Meet Allan Bott

Hi, I’m Allan Bott, founder of Managed Assets.

I’m an IT Asset, Licence and Vendor Management specialist with over 10 years of experience in the IT industry and more than 8 years in contract management and commercial negotiation.

Throughout my career, I’ve helped organisations optimise software licensing, reduce audit risk, and negotiate better supplier agreements.

Throughout my career, I have delivered measurable financial and operational benefits, including:

  • Over $300,000 in software licence and contract savings over a 24-month period through licence harvesting, contract renegotiation, and vendor rationalisation.
  • Successful support for £130 million of contract renewals and re-bids.
  • A £60,000 efficiency saving on a major infrastructure project.
  • Development of KPI and assurance frameworks to improve governance and performance.

These achievements demonstrate my ability to combine technical asset management expertise with strong commercial and negotiation skills to deliver tangible business value.
